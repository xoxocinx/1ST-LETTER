To you,

Okay, first of all, congratulations! You've officially received a letter. HAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHA. I don't know if you're supposed to feel special or slightly confused, but either way, I hope this makes you smile 😁✌

I wanted to write something for you, not because there's a special occasion, but simply because I felt like you deserved something a little different. Besides, when was the last time someone actually sat down and wrote you a letter? Exactly. Let me have this moment.

I hope you know how lovely it is to get to know you. I like hearing your stories, listening to your random thoughts, and discovering little things about you that somehow make me appreciate you even more. Even your pagka-goofy, your random reactions, and those moments when bigla kang nagiging super talkative. They're little things, but they're the kind I find myself remembering.

And about your overthinking, I hope you won't hesitate to tell me whenever something is bothering you. Kahit pa feeling mo ang liit lang ng bagay or you're thinking about something over and over again. You don't have to figure out how to explain everything perfectly before telling me. If there's something you need reassurance about, just tell me. I'd rather understand what's going on in your mind kaysa hayaan kang mag-alala ng mag-isa. I know we're still getting to know each other, and there's still a lot for us to learn, but I hope we can build that kind of comfort between us.

And one more thing, you don't have to apologize for every little thing, okay? You don't have to feel guilty for being tired, needing time, or having days when you can't give your best. You're allowed to be human, too. Hindi mo kailangang laging mag-explain or bumawi sa mga bagay na hindi mo naman kasalanan.

I also want you to know that I don't just want to know the version of you that laughs at my jokes or tells me about the good things happening in your day. I want to understand you on the quieter days, too. You don't have to pretend everything is fine just to keep our conversations light. You can tell me when something feels heavy, even if you don't know exactly how to put it into words. I'll do my best to listen and understand.

And please, don't be too hard on yourself. You don't have to be at your best all the time, nor do you have to make yourself smaller just because you think you're being too much. You can be silly, quiet, emotional, makulit, or whatever version of yourself shows up that day. You don't have to filter every little part of you.

On another note, I hope you never lose that part of yourself that finds happiness in dancing. I love seeing how passionate you are about something that means so much to you. I hope you continue pursuing the things that make you feel alive, even as life gets busier. And I hope you remember to take care of yourself along the way, too. You deserve to enjoy the things you love without forgetting to give yourself time to rest.

I won't pretend that I already know every chapter of your story. We're still getting there, after all. But I look forward to discovering more of them, one conversation at a time. No need to rush through the pages, I'd rather read them properly than skip straight to the ending.

And lastly, if you ever find yourself wondering what makes you worth appreciating, I hope you remember that you don't always have to do something extraordinary to be special. Sometimes, simply being yourself is already more than enough.

Also, don't get used to receiving letters every day. I have a reputation to maintain HAHAHAHA. Kidding aside, I hope this is something you'll look back on and smile about someday.

From someone who's genuinely glad to have met you 🫶
